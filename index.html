How it's made

From plant to dropper

STEP 1

Harvest & dry

Herbs are cut at peak potency and air-dried out of direct light.

STEP 2

Steep

Plant matter sits in alcohol or oil for 2–6 weeks, shaken daily.

STEP 3

Strain & press

The spent herb is pressed out, keeping every drop of the good stuff.

STEP 4

Bottle & label

Hand-labeled with the batch number and the day it was bottled.
